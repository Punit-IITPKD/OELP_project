## 1. A Survey of Methods for Time Series Change Point Detection
- URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5464762/
- Date: 2017 (published in Knowledge and Information Systems; PMC record).
- Snippet: “A typical statistical formulation of change-point detection is to analyze the probability distributions of data before and after a candidate change point, and identify the candidate as a change point if the two distributions are significantly different.”

## 5. Recovering the Underlying Trajectory from Sparse and Irregular Longitudinal Data
- URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1809.06462
- Date: 2018 (arXiv identifier 1809.06462; version/date not shown in result excerpt).
- Snippet: PACE is described as a popular approach for sparse and irregular longitudinal data; it may be numerically unstable because it involves inversion of a covariance matrix. The paper proposes a sparse orthonormal approximation.
